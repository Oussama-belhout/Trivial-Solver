  ⚠ Three findings to address BEFORE paying for Haiku / R1                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                              
  1. first_shot_success = 0% across all 30 runs — almost certainly an off-by-one bug

  30/30 problems showing exactly 1 refinement is too clean to be honest measurement. Iteration counter is likely being incremented by
  monolith_model_node itself (so even a first-try success registers as iter=1). If this isn't fixed, the first-shot trajectory in the L0→L4
  plot will be flat-zero everywhere — destroys one of your headline metrics.

  2. CoT is broken — 30% compile errors, all [3,1] class, interface, enum, or record expected

  That error means non-Java content leaked into the top of the .java file. Symptom: _strip_think_block() isn't catching every
  <think>...</think> shape Qwen emits (probably unclosed tags or alternative delimiters). Don't run CoT on Haiku/R1 until fixed — would waste
  money on a known-broken variant.

  3. solution: {} empty + faithfulness 0% (pre-existing)

  Bridge isn't returning variable assignments. Lower priority — Q1 (success rate) is unaffected; Q2 (faithfulness) is expected to be poor at
  L0 anyway. Can fix before L4 runs.
